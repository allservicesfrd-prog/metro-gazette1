Editorial Guidelines & Standards

Metro-Gazette strictly adheres to the fundamental principles of modern journalism: accuracy, fairness, impartiality, and transparency.

1. Verification & Fact-Checking

Every story published on Metro-Gazette undergoes rigorous verification. We utilize official press releases, primary documentation, direct statements, and reliable public records to cross-reference facts before publication. Unverified rumors or unsourced claims are explicitly excluded from our news reporting.

2. AP Style & Inverted Pyramid Standards

Our news articles are structured using the traditional Inverted Pyramid model following Associated Press (AP) style guidelines. Crucial facts—Who, What, When, Where, and Why—are presented upfront in the lead, followed by essential contextual details, supporting evidence, and background information.

3. Technology & Production Process

To deliver rapid breaking coverage without compromising quality, Metro-Gazette integrates cutting-edge digital newsroom tools into its editorial workflow. We utilize advanced AI research assistants to summarize primary sources, analyze trends, and assist in translation across languages. However, all content undergoes human editorial review, oversight, and curation prior to publication to ensure factual accuracy and alignment with our standards.

4. Corrections & Updates Policy

Accuracy is our highest priority. If an error of fact is identified in a published article, Metro-Gazette promptly corrects the text and appends a clear correction note stating what was updated and when.

Ownership & Transparency

Metro-Gazette is an independent media outlet owned and operated by Francky Delius. We operate free from political affiliation or hidden commercial influence. Our revenue is generated primarily through digital advertising, allowing us to maintain free access to news for all readers.
